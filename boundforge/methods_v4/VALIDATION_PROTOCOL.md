Frozen before first run: masks.py from 5db40cd899de482cd1be217255eed2acc9eae9f5; independent_masks.py/validate_masks.py in the committing revision.

16 development-adjacent but prospectively generated fixtures: seed902710, d2 andd3, 3 sampled old points in [-2,2]^d, 4 rational integer projections. These are not broad task transfer or a sealed confirmatory discovery benchmark. Two previously exposed archived C3c supports test realistic scale, separately identified.

For each, compare the full mask set from exact affine-flat enumeration against independent Z3 QF_LRA satisfiability of every Boolean novelty assignment. Solver unknown/time limit is unresolved, never unsatisfiable. Rational models are rechecked directly without importing the geometry compiler.

Primary rejection: any fully resolved mask-set disagreement. Secondary diagnostic: total CPU including construction, not just membership lookup. This is also a strong simpler exact baseline. If symbolic enumeration dominates, narrow any claim of an efficient new oracle. Z3 and arrangement traversal are standard methods. No statistical speed superiority claim from one pass; retain per-task timings and solver/version hashes.

Budget: 3sec wall solver timeout per mask, 30CPU seconds per task before stopping additional checks, Actions timeout20min. No GPU/model/training. Both procedures see identical inputs. This does not test a learned controller, record discovery, or a new general method.
