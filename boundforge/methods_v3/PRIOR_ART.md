These sources substantially narrow the original hypothesis. The immediate experiment tests a cost-sensitive probing mechanism on harder exactly checkable tasks. It is exploratory development; none of its outcomes will be relabeled untouched confirmation. Existing C49 development/evaluation tasks are exposed and are not reused as held-out evidence.
