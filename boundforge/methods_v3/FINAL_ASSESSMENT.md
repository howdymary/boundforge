## Direct answers to the requested contribution questions

1. **What exactly is new?** New project code, exact environment adapters, certificates and negative experimental evidence. A new general research mechanism is not established.
2. **Relative to which prior work?** The mechanisms overlap with adaptive search, arrangement enumeration, parametric optimization, proof reuse and exact caching. The sourced comparison above prevents a broader claim.
3. **What evidence shows it helps?** Shared algebra saves repeated elimination and exact event scans improve some construction-search results. The proposed diagnostic/memory mechanisms lack confirmed advantage over strong simpler controls.
4. **What simpler explanations were ruled out?** The paired-credit distinction was disproved algebraically; stronger rebuild/common-library/coordinate-event/cache controls exposed weaker explanations. Extra tooling, source access, retrieval or validity throughput cannot establish novelty.
5. **Where does it transfer?** Shared-signature algebra was functionally checked on generic grouped forms in dimensions3/5/7. Event scans were tested on related planar projection tasks; weighted production used a separate numerical adapter. This is not held-out evidence of broad discovery transfer.
6. **Where does it fail?** Diagnostic overhead, weak coupling between feasible repairs and objective gain, greedy stagnation, and cache overhead can dominate. Higher-nullity scans are incomplete slices. Model proposals repeatedly confused standard mechanisms or metrics with novelty.
7. **What mathematical discovery did it enable?** No new record in this campaign. Earlier C51a/C3c refinements retain their original chronology and limitations.
8. **Which Track1 criteria are actually satisfied?** Autonomous bounded loops and inspectable verification/logging. A new record from the new method and a defensible new-method advantage remain unmet.

The unresolved research question is whether a mathematically justified transformation can propose interventions *outside* an already certified neighborhood and predict useful gains across genuinely different construction families, without reducing to conventional operator synthesis, feasible mutation or cached exact search. That question currently lacks a materially distinct, test-ready mechanism. Repeating the rejected pilots would not answer it.
